About Us

Built around dependable supply, technical capability and long-term partnerships.

Petroleum Doha Trading & Contracting W.L.L. is a Qatar-based trading and contracting organization delivering specialized supply and support solutions across key industrial sectors.

Company Overview

Supply and support for demanding industrial requirements.

The company sources and supplies OEM spare parts, engineered materials and MRO solutions supporting upstream, midstream and downstream facilities. Our supply chain framework emphasizes traceability, technical compliance, quality assurance, documentation accuracy, on-time delivery and responsive after-sales support.

Company Overview

Supply and execution under one roof

Petroleum Doha Trading & Contracting is a Qatar-based trading and contracting company operating under Al Baida Holding. The company provides trading, OEM spare parts, MRO solutions, engineered materials, equipment supply and rental, and contracting and technical services.

We serve the Oil & Gas, Energy, EPC & Industrial, Marine & Offshore, Aviation, Healthcare, Facility Management and Hospitality sectors — supporting operators, contractors and facility owners with specification-driven sourcing and dependable site delivery.

Our capabilities bring supply, fabrication, installation, maintenance and site support together to help clients coordinate demanding project and operational requirements.
